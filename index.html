Dual 501(c)(3) & 501(c)(7) Notice

Official Fraternal Notice & Tax Status: Mu Theta Lambda Alumni Chapter is an unincorporated fraternal association operating under Section 501(c)(7) of the Internal Revenue Code.

Charitable contributions, scholarship donations, and educational program grants are disbursed through the Mu Theta Lambda Educational & Charitable Foundation, Inc., a tax-exempt public charity recognized under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code (EIN: 58-2940192). Contributions to the Foundation are tax-deductible to the fullest extent permitted by federal law.

National Organization Disclaimer: Mu Theta Lambda Alumni Chapter is a separately incorporated local affiliate governed in compliance with its national fraternal constitution and bylaws. The trademarks, crests, and insignia are property of their respective national organization.
